# 🎬 Echo - 2-Minute Judging Demo Script

Follow this step-by-step 2-minute path to demonstrate all 4 phases of Echo to hackathon judges:

---

### Step 1: Landing Page & Problem Pitch (0:00 - 0:25)
1. Open `http://localhost:5173/`.
2. Point out the hero badge, bold headline, and **two CTAs** (*"Start a Free Room"*, *"Explore Live Recap Demo"*).
3. Scroll through the **Problem Section** (*Why live voice conversations vanish*) and show the standard vs. Echo contrast card.

---

### Step 2: Interest Onboarding & Feed Recommendations (0:25 - 0:45)
1. Click **Onboarding** in the sticky navbar (`/onboarding`).
2. Select interests: **Placements** and **AI & CS**. Select languages: **English** and **Hindi**. Click **Complete Onboarding**.
3. Point out the ranked feed on `/home` showing the explainable formula label: `"Recommended because you like Placements"`.

---

### Step 3: Live Room Experience (0:45 - 1:25)
1. Navigate to `/rooms` and click **Join Stage** or click **Start a Room**.
2. Inside `/room/spontaneous-room`:
   - Point out **Host / Speaker / Listener** roles.
   - Click floating reaction emojis (👏 💡 ❓ 🔥) to watch them float up the screen.
   - Click **📌 Pin Moment** to save a timestamped bookmark.
   - Speak into your mic or click **Speak** to demonstrate live Web Speech API captions.
   - Vote on the **Live Poll** widget and reveal percentage bar charts.
   - Change the **Language Dropdown** to *Spanish* to show machine translation.

---

### Step 4: End Room & Generated Recap (1:25 - 1:45)
1. Click **End Room & Generate Recap**.
2. On `/recap/recap-101`:
   - Show the generated **Executive Summary**, **Key Points** with clickable timestamps jumping to transcript moments, and **Action Items**.
   - Show the **"Continue This Discussion"** section with auto-generated follow-up rooms.
   - Click **Schedule This Room 1-Click**.

---

### Step 5: Searchable Library & 1-on-1 Match (1:45 - 2:00)
1. Click **Recap Library** (`/library`) and type `"roadmap"` in the search bar to filter recaps by keyword.
2. Click **1-on-1 Match** (`/match`), select topic *Placements*, click **Match with Demo Peer**, and test instant voice matching!
